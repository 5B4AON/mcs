Farnsworth timing sends individual characters at full speed but stretches the gaps between characters and words, lowering the effective overall speed. This is a proven learning technique named after Donald R. “Russ” Farnsworth (W6TTB) that helps you recognise characters at real speed while giving you extra thinking time.
