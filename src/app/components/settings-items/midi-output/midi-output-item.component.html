Characters from any input are played as morse elements (dits/dahs) at Encoder WPM. Straight Key fires on every element. Paddle fires dit/dah notes separately. Both straight-key and element-specific notes fire simultaneously.
By default the MIDI output repeats the timing of the morse it receives, so morse arriving at 20 WPM leaves at 20 WPM. Enable this to play everything at your own Encoder WPM instead. This is useful when you receive morse at one speed (for example from a fast sender or a relay input) and want the connected MIDI device to play it back at a different, slower or faster, speed.
