Forwards decoded text to a K1EL WinKeyer (WK2/WK3/WKUSB) device. WinKeyer generates perfectly timed CW keying on its output — the host sends plain ASCII characters and WinKeyer handles the morse timing internally.

Connection: 1200 baud, 8N2. The WinKeyer appears as a virtual COM port (USB-serial). Select which decoded text to forward: RX (received), TX (transmitted by the encoder/keyer), or both.

Use case: Re-key received or transmitted morse through a WinKeyer for relay, practice, or driving an external transmitter with clean timing.
