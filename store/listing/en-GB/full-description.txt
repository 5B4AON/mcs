Send, receive and practise Morse code with a browser-based studio for learning, teaching, demonstrations and CW operation.

SEND MORSE
Type text and hear it as accurately timed Morse audio. Choose Send on Enter or Live, and set the sending speed, Farnsworth spacing and sidetone to suit you.

KEY YOUR OWN MORSE
Key with the keyboard, mouse or touchscreen: straight key or iambic paddles (Iambic A or B, Ultimatic, single lever) with an on-screen key that animates as you send. Your keying is decoded and shown as text. MIDI controllers work where the browser and device support them. USB-serial and WinKeyer support is unavailable on Android. Keyboard and touch keying need the app in the foreground.

DECODE CW
Listen to incoming Morse through the microphone, or load a recording from your device, and decode it in real time. A scope shows the signal, and the decoder finds the pitch and speed by itself. Save the decoded text as a file.

LEARN AND PRACTISE
- Copy Practice: listen to random characters, words or callsigns, or to your own text or an e-book, and check what you copied.
- Send Practice: key a prompt and get accuracy and speed feedback.
- Koch method: 40 progressive lessons with new characters explained as they sound.
- Blurred text and Type-along modes to build real copying skills.

SEE IT YOUR WAY
Open the full-screen conversation view with your choice of fonts, colours and sizes, or use Lens to show one character with its dots and dashes. A distraction-free vintage typewriter look is available too.

GUIDED SETUP AND PRESETS
The Setup Wizard walks you through common goals at your own experience level, and a Teach guide helps teachers prepare a class session. Save your settings as named presets and share them as files.

OPTIONAL ONLINE RELAY
The optional relay lets devices exchange Morse characters over the internet. It is off by default and needs an internet connection. When enabled, it sends each new character with the name, colour and speed you choose. Do not send personal information through the relay.

PRIVACY
No account is required. The app has no ads, analytics or tracking. Microphone audio and loaded recordings are processed on your device and are not recorded or uploaded. Permissions are requested only after you choose a feature that needs one. Read the privacy policy at https://morse-code-studio.web.app/privacy.html.

Morse Code Studio is intended for ages 13 and over. Younger learners should use it with a teacher, parent or club leader.

The app needs an internet connection to load. After it has loaded and cached, core features can work offline; the relay and other online features need a connection.
